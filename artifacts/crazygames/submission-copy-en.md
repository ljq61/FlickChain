# FlickChain — English submission copy

## Title

FlickChain

## Short description

Flick coins, build a chain, and keep your streak alive on a cozy 3D tabletop.

## Description

Line up your shot, charge your flick, and hit exactly one other coin directly. Score a point, then take your next shot from the coin you just hit. Other coins can knock into each other, but a miss, a direct hit on multiple coins, or any coin falling off the table breaks the chain.

Play solo to beat your best score, with three continues to keep your run going. Each completed round brings more coins to the table. Or play against a friend on the same device or challenge the computer in a race to 10 points. Before a match, each player takes an opening shot—the longest shot that comes to rest on the table goes first.

## Controls

- Click or tap a coin to select it. You can change your starting coin before shooting.
- Drag empty space to line up your shot.
- Press and hold the finger behind the selected coin to charge. Hold longer for more power, then release to flick.
- Release within two seconds of reaching full charge, or the shot is canceled.
- Select the gear button to pause, resume, restart, or return to the main menu.
- Select the speaker button to turn sound on or off. CrazyGames can also mute the game.

## Suggested categories

Casual / Skill / Physics / 3D / 2 Player

These are submission suggestions, not confirmed portal taxonomy selections. The game uses pointer/touch input and supports portrait and landscape layouts. Local two-player play uses one shared device; it is not online multiplayer.
